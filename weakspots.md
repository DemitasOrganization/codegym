# Weakspots

Recurring misses, updated by the skill when a pattern is confirmed across
sessions (not from a single bad day). Read each morning to bias concept
selection toward spaced re-surfacing. An entry clears when the user
demonstrably resolves it.

This is where spaced repetition gets teeth: without it, "drill the thing you
keep flubbing" lives only in one conversation's memory. Keep it small — a
handful of live items, not an archive.

Ships **empty**.

Format: `- [track] concept — why it keeps biting (date noticed)`
